# FixIt promo video – scripts

Length: about 75 seconds, 8 scenes. The same visuals are used for both languages; only the voice and on-screen captions change.
Format: 1920×1080 (16:9) for YouTube and LinkedIn, plus an optional 1080×1080 square cut for the LinkedIn feed.
Visuals: real FixIt UI (button, preview card, Reply window) animated on top of mock chat boxes. Other companies' logos are never shown; app names appear as plain text.

**Description:** Tired of copy-pasting into ChatGPT to fix your messages? FixIt adds a "Fix grammar" button to every text box on Windows: Fiverr, Gmail, WhatsApp, Slack. Preview the changes, change the style, translate Bangla to English, and draft client replies with Ctrl+Shift+R. Runs on free AI (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter).
